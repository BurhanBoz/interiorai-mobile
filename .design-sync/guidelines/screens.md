# Screens (app version 2.6.1, 20 Oct 2026)

Reference images of the current app are in `reference/` (iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator, 660 × 1434). Every image here is a real screen; every room picture in them is a real output of the app.

## Onboarding — `reference/current-onboarding-1.png`, `current-onboarding-2.png`
Three pages, swiped. Each page is a full-height before/after of the same living room with a gold handle that sweeps on its own (Halloween → Cozy Christmas → an empty room furnished). "ROOMFRAME" lockup top-left, pager dots, a one-line serif headline ("Your room, ready for Halloween"), a gold NEXT / START button, "Skip" under it. Shown once per install; START creates a guest account silently and lands on the Studio.

## Studio (home) — `reference/current-studio.png`
"ROOMFRAME" lockup and a credit badge (★ 2) at the top; kicker "WHAT SHALL WE MAKE?". One wide season tile (Halloween, before/after split, three theme thumbnails, ✦ NEW tag) and six tool tiles in two columns, each showing the tool's real result with the "before" as a small inset: Redesign the room, Furnish an empty room, Replace or remove a piece, Keep a piece redo the rest, Copy this look (PRO), Garden & exterior (PRO). Tab bar: Studio · Gallery · Settings. (`current-studio-old.png` is the pre-2.5 Studio for comparison.)

## Add your room — `reference/current-add-room.png`
Back chevron + tool name. The tool's own before/after with a handle (230 pt). "ADD YOUR ROOM": two doors side by side — Take a photo (gold wash, recommended) and Choose from library. "OR TRY WITH A SAMPLE ROOM": three sample thumbnails (Living room · Kitchen · Dining room).

## Sample "designing" — `reference/current-sample-designing.png`
Tapping a sample plays the real generation screen for ten seconds: the room blurred full-screen, the sharp photo in a gold-edged card with a scan line, "Designing your living room", "<tool> · about a minute", a thin gold progress bar. Then the result.

## Sample result — `reference/current-sample-result.png`, `current-sample-result-halloween.png`
The sample's finished before/after (tall, with the handle), kicker "NOW YOUR OWN ROOM", the same two doors (camera / library), tab bar visible.

## Composer (after a photo)
The user's photo on top with a "Change photo" pill. Sections: "Which room is it?" (horizontal strip of room-type photos), "Pick a style" (strip of style thumbnails, selected ringed in gold), "Advanced" (palette, intensity), "Place a real piece" furniture strip. Bottom: summary line (room · pieces), the credit cost ("1 credit"), a gold GENERATE button.

## Generation — like `current-sample-designing.png`
The user's own room blurred behind the scan card; ~60–90 s. The notification question appears here once.

## Result — `reference/current-intro-redesign.png` shows the slider style
The design full-width with the before/after handle; Save · Share; "Another style" strip; the rating question keyed to a value signal. The Pro offer opens after the SECOND finished result.

## Paywall — `reference/current-paywall-feature.png`
Full-bleed hero (the tool's or the user's own before/after, inset "BEFORE" thumbnail), kicker "ROOMFRAME PRO", serif headline, one-line value, two plan rows (Pro · Weekly selected by default: $5.99 first week then $7.99/week, −25% badge; Pro · Monthly: $20.99 first month then $26.99/month, −22%), gold "START PRO · $5.99 today", the renewal sentence, Restore · Terms · Privacy, close X top-right. On "out of credits" the hero is the user's own last design and the headline "Your next room is one tap away", with a one-time pack row under the plans.
