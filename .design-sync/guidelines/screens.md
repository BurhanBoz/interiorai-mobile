# Screens

Reference images of the current app are in `reference/`.

## Studio (home) — `reference/current-studio.png`
"ROOMFRAME" lockup and credit badge at the top. A large gold "Add photo" tile is the one primary action, beside two sample rooms ("Try the sample"). Below, feature tiles with real photos: Redesign, Empty Room, Magic Edit (small), Style Transfer and Outdoor Design (large, marked PRO). Tab bar: Studio · Gallery · Settings.

## Options — `reference/current-options.png`
The user's photo at the top with a room-type chip ("Living room · change") and "Change" on photo chrome. A horizontal carousel of style thumbnails (Modern, Minimalist, Scandinavian, Industrial, …), the selected one ringed in gold. An "Advanced" row (palette, intensity, keep layout). A "Put a real piece in" furniture strip. Bottom: "Modern · Living room" summary with the credit cost, and a full-width gold "CREATE" button.

## Progress
The user's own room, blurred, behind a calm progress state; the design takes about a minute. The notification permission question ("Want a reminder?") appears here on the first design.

## Result
The generated image with a before/after slider. Actions: Save · Share · Room video · Try another style (a strip of styles). The first result opens the paywall once, using the user's own before/after.

## Paywall — `reference/current-paywall.png`
Kicker "ROOMFRAME", serif headline "Redesign every room", one-line value, the before/after image, two plan rows (weekly selected by default, with a discount badge), a full-width gold "START PRO" button with the price, the renewal terms, and Restore · Terms · Privacy.
