# Brand

- **Name:** Roomframe AI. Lockup: "ROOMFRAME" in Inter SemiBold, uppercase, tracking `--rf-track-brand`, often in `--rf-gold` or `--rf-ink-muted`. Logo in `reference/logo.png`.
- **Voice:** an interior architect, calm and editorial. Short sentences, concrete nouns (sofa, rug, daylight), no exclamation marks.
- **Look:** warm umber dark ground, golden-hour gold accent, serif headlines over photography. Imagery is real-looking interior photography — the product's output — never illustrations or 3D renders.
- **Photography:** the room is the hero; chrome over photos stays small and translucent (`--rf-photo-chrome`).
