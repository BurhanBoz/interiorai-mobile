# First-run brief — what to redesign and why (owner, 20 Oct 2026)

The owner wants **radical** proposals for everything between install and the first finished design. The screen-discipline rule in `product-rules.md` ("keep the current layout logic") is **lifted for this flow**; the other rules (paywall law, no custom rating widget, credits never shown as money, 10 languages, 44 pt targets) still hold.

## The numbers behind the brief (24 Aug – 19 Oct, real users)
- **62 % of installs never make a single design.** 22 of 24 non-makers opened the app once, for ~86 seconds. Everyone who did make a design did it inside the first 10 minutes. The decision happens in the first 90 seconds.
- **Only 13 % of first designs are followed by a second** (when the free allowance was 1 credit); 53–57 % when it was 3 or 2/day. 8 of the 10 paying customers made at least one design before buying.
- The out-of-credits screen was shown 44 times in 14 days and nobody picked a plan. People who reached Apple's price sheet from the credit badge cancelled 8 times out of 8.
- 7 of 10 subscribers were weekly and left after the first week.

## Goal of every proposal
**The user sees their OWN room redesigned within 90 seconds of opening the app**, with the fewest taps and decisions. Then one obvious step to a second design.

## What to propose (one artboard each, 390 × 844)
1. **Onboarding as one screen.** A single full-screen before/after (the handle sweeps itself), one headline, one button that opens the camera/library directly. No pager, no skip, no second page. The guest account is created in the background.
2. **Studio on the first session.** One decision: add a room. The six tools and the season appear as a quiet strip or after the first result — not seven equal tiles. Show what the same Studio looks like on the second session (the full grid) as a smaller companion board if useful.
3. **Composer with one tap to Generate.** The room type is pre-filled by the server's read of the photo; a recommended style is pre-selected and the strip shows alternatives; "Advanced" is folded away; the credit line is hidden on the first design. The button is the only gold element.
4. **Result → second design.** Under the slider, one large gold action "Try another style on this photo" that re-runs the same photo with a different style — no return to the Studio, no re-upload. Save/Share secondary.
5. **Out of credits.** The user's own last before/after as the hero, headline "Your next room is one tap away", plans with **monthly selected by default**, the button reading the period plainly ("$20.99 first month · cancel anytime"), the one-time pack as a quiet row.

## Keep
- Dark umber ground, gold accent, serif headlines, the before/after handle as the signature interaction, real room photographs only.
- The ten-second "designing" screen on sample rooms (already shipped) and the real generation screen's scan card.

## Do not propose
- Light theme (not now), step labels, explainer cards, confetti, fake timers, credit counts turned into prices.
